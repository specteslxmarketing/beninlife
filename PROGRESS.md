## Notes
- Calls use public STUN (`stun:stun.l.google.com:19302`). Production needs a TURN server for strict NATs.
- getUserMedia needs HTTPS (or localhost). Over plain HTTP a call still connects, but listen-only.
- In this sandbox, Chrome sees **no network interfaces** (0 ICE host candidates, even for an in-page loopback test). The
  headless e2e therefore checks signalling and the full SDP offer/answer negotiation; audio itself cannot flow on this box.
- Car and house prices are in-game estimates in ₦, not market quotes. Model names are generic "-style" labels (no real brand logos).
- E2E runs use the real local database. Combat, clothes and iPad-landscape e2e use separate per-run test accounts (funded with `admin:grant`). Older scripts (houses, car stands, travel, police, jobs, weather, vehicle, characters, ambient, iPad portrait) still log in as `bestx` for admin teleport and can spend small amounts; the crown reserve puts him back to ₦5bn+ within 15 s.
- NPCs are deliberately few (owner's steering): 2 mansion guards, 4 checkpoint police officers, the plane advisor, a cabin attendant and 3 seated passengers, one taxi passenger at a time, and one customer car per job shift. The world is meant to be filled by real players.
- Lagos is placed at world x≈2000 (out of sight of Benin). The camera far plane is 1,400 m in Benin and 800 m in Lagos. The ground plane is subdivided because a huge 2-triangle plane lost depth precision out there.
- Ambient traffic and pedestrians are cosmetic and simulated on each client (not server-synced); only the traffic-light phase is shared.
- Wanted level is stored on the server (`users.wanted`), so logging out does not clear it.
- Combat realism limits: no shooting from vehicles (drive-bys); police responders follow the road graph and go straight once they have line of sight (no full navmesh pathfinding); walls for line of sight are the static building colliders (props and parked cars do not stop bullets).
- Movement is procedural (no CC0 motion-capture clips were usable); it blends walk/run/strafe/aim/lying poses.
- Performance: the Benin scene is still about 4,800 draw calls / 2M triangles at Low. Car LOD + merging halved it, but the ~680 world props are not merged yet. The e2e browsers use software GL (swiftshader, often under 1 fps), so tests run at Low and only draw a page when taking a screenshot (game logic keeps running).
